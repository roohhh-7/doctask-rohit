# Task 3: Target Buyer Roles & Door-Opener Conversation Strategies

> **Outreach Rule**: *We do all outreach ourselves, always. This document maps buyer psychology, listening channels, and door-opening hooks for future GTM execution.*

---

## 1. Federal / Defense Proposal Operations
- **Target Buyer Role**: *Director of Proposal Operations / Head of Capture Strategy* (e.g. Booz Allen, CACI, Leidos).
- **Where They Listen**:
  - APMP (Association of Proposal Management Professionals) conferences & regional chapters.
  - GovCon LinkedIn groups & GovBrew / Washington Technology industry roundups.
- **Why Existing Tools Fail Them**: Google Docs and Word have no semantic understanding of compliance matrices; standard ChatGPT destroys table layout formatting and lacks in-document diff tracking.
- **Door-Opening Conversation Hook**:
  > *"When an RFP amendment changes technical specs 12 hours before deadline, proposal teams spend half the night manually checking if Section 3.4 contradicts the Section 8 budget narrative. We built an in-document AI editor that finds all cross-section parameter dependencies across a 120-page document and executes targeted updates in-place with redline diff review. Would you be open to seeing a 90-second side-by-side run on a sample volume?"*

---

## 2. Clinical Research & Regulatory Affairs
- **Target Buyer Role**: *VP of Regulatory Affairs / Director of Medical Writing* (e.g. Medpace, ICON, Parexel).
- **Where They Listen**:
  - DIA (Drug Information Association) annual meetings.
  - American Medical Writers Association (AMWA) forums and BioSpace technical blogs.
- **Why Existing Tools Fail Them**: Medical writers refuse to copy patient data or clinical protocols into web chat windows due to context window truncation, formatting destruction, and loss of medical citation anchors.
- **Door-Opening Conversation Hook**:
  > *"Medical writers typically lose 15+ hours per protocol amendment just reconciling primary endpoint descriptions across the Synopsis, Study Design, and Statistical Analysis Plan. SuperDocs acts like Cursor for clinical documents—allowing your team to highlight disjoint sections across a 90-page protocol and execute synchronized, citation-preserving edits directly in the file. Here is an interactive preview of how it handles endpoint amendments."*

## 3. Commercial Due Diligence & Strategy Consulting
- **Target Buyer Role**: *Senior Practice Director (Private Equity) / Knowledge Operations Partner* (e.g. Bain, L.E.K.).
- **Where They Listen**:
  - Private Equity International (PEI) briefings, Wall Street Oasis private equity channels, consulting alumni Slack networks.
- **Why Existing Tools Fail Them**: Consultants working late night before investment committee meetings cannot risk inconsistent market sizing CAGR numbers between the Executive Summary and Sector deep dives.
- **Door-Opening Conversation Hook**:
  > *"During a 3-week commercial diligence sprint, when market growth projections are revised from 12% to 15%, case teams manually scan 50 pages to ensure no stale numbers slip into the final pack. We built an in-document environment where you update the primary finding once, and it propagates synchronized, surgical updates across the entire memorandum with full version history and revert capability."*

---

## 4. Architecture & Engineering Project Delivery
- **Target Buyer Role**: *Director of Specifications / Head of Project Delivery* (e.g. Gensler, AECOM).
- **Where They Listen**:
  - AIA (American Institute of Architects) Practice Management Knowledge Community, CSI (Construction Specifications Institute) technical symposiums.
- **Why Existing Tools Fail Them**: MasterFormat CSI specification packs are massive and multi-disciplinary; rich-text editors lack multi-document targeted section referencing.
- **Door-Opening Conversation Hook**:
  > *"When sustainability specs or LEED targets change during design development, propagating those updates across fixture schedules, MEP narratives, and architectural specs is prone to expensive site omission errors. SuperDocs provides multi-document targeted editing that aligns technical specifications directly in the project pack."*
