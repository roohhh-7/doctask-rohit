# Task 3: Researched Companies & Document Friction Profiles

> **Rule Compliance Note**: *Contains strictly public corporate research. In accordance with round rules, zero named individuals and zero personal contact details are stored.*

---

## 1. Booz Allen Hamilton (Defense & GovTech Consulting)
- **What They Do**: Major government contractor delivering technical consulting, cyber operations, and defense AI systems.
- **Why This Bites Them**: Submits hundreds of multi-million dollar federal RFPs annually. Proposals exceed 100 pages with rigid Section L/M compliance matrices. When technical parameters change 4 hours before the submission deadline, proposal managers must manually comb through 12 separate volumes to update references.
- **Target Role Inside**: *Vice President of Proposal Operations / Director of Capture Management*.

---

## 2. Medpace (Clinical Research Organization)
- **What They Do**: Full-service clinical research organization managing Phase I–IV clinical trials for biotech sponsors.
- **Why This Bites Them**: Prepares FDA Clinical Study Reports (CSRs) and Investigator Brochures. Protocol amendments require updating primary endpoint descriptions, patient exclusion criteria, and statistical power calculations simultaneously across 6 different sections.
- **Target Role Inside**: *Director of Medical Writing / VP of Regulatory Affairs*.

---

## 3. Bain & Company (Management Consulting)
- **What They Do**: Global management consultancy advising private equity and Fortune 500 leadership.
- **Why This Bites Them**: Commercial due diligence (CDD) reports are produced under extreme 2-to-3 week timeframes. As primary market research data trickles in, case teams must continually revise market sizing estimates, CAGR figures, and customer churn metrics across 40+ slides/document pages.
- **Target Role Inside**: *Senior Practice Director (Private Equity Group) / Head of Knowledge Operations*.

---

## 4. Vanta (Automated Compliance & Security)
- **What They Do**: Security and compliance automation platform helping companies achieve SOC-2, ISO 27001, and HIPAA certification.
- **Why This Bites Them**: Generates and maintains hundreds of standardized yet company-specific security policy documents. When a client modifies their cloud architecture (e.g. migrating from AWS to GCP), policy suites require surgical parameter updates across 20+ policy documents.
- **Target Role Inside**: *Head of GRC Solutions / Lead Compliance Architect*.

---

## 5. Gensler (Architecture, Design & Planning)
- **What They Do**: World's largest architecture firm designing commercial, institutional, and urban developments.
- **Why This Bites Them**: Project handover packs and technical specifications (MasterFormat CSI specifications) bridge architectural intent, MEP engineering, and structural codes. Any change in material specs requires immediate cross-referencing across sustainability schedules and contractor handovers.
- **Target Role Inside**: *Director of Specifications & Quality / Head of Project Delivery*.

---

## 6. Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard (Biomedical Research)
- **What They Do**: World-leading biomedical genomics institute producing cutting-edge genomic tools and clinical datasets.
- **Why This Bites Them**: Massive multi-institutional grant applications (NIH U01, P50 centers) written by 8–15 co-investigators. Managing bibliography reconciliation and experimental protocol synchronization across disparate academic writing styles is a massive administrative bottleneck.
- **Target Role Inside**: *Director of Sponsored Research Administration / Lead Principal Investigator*.

---

## 7. Spencer Stuart (Executive Search & Leadership Advisory)
- **What They Do**: Global executive search firm assessing and placing C-suite executives and board directors.
- **Why This Bites Them**: Produces confidential candidate appraisal dossiers (25–35 pages per finalist). Partners synthesize interview transcripts, psychometric evaluations, and reference notes into highly tailored comparative executive briefing books.
- **Target Role Inside**: *Global Practice Leader / Head of Executive Assessment Delivery*.

---

## 8. Palantir Technologies (Enterprise Software & AI Platforms)
- **What They Do**: Builds mission-critical data platforms (Gotham, Foundry, AIP) for government and commercial enterprises.
- **Why This Bites Them**: Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) write comprehensive technical architecture RFCs and customer deployment runbooks. Code snippets, API contracts, and security boundaries must stay strictly in sync during customer POC sprints.
- **Target Role Inside**: *Lead Forward Deployed Architect / Director of Technical Field Engineering*.
