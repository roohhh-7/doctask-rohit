## 3. Core Feature Pillars for Technical Teams

### 1. Section-Precision Multi-Edit
Prompt: *"In Section 2, update training dataset to 50k samples. Recalculate Section 5 compute timeline and adjust the H100 budget row in the Section 7 table."*  
SuperDocs edits all three sections simultaneously, keeping the document perfectly in sync.

### 2. Lossless LaTeX, Word & PDF Fidelity
Imports `.tex` project `.zip` archives with real footnotes, bibliography keys, and equations that export to native Word math. Word documents with **Track Changes** import cleanly with author attribution preserved.

### 3. Human-in-the-Loop Diff Approval
Every proposed change arrives as a clean, granular before/after diff. You stay in complete control of your scientific record.
