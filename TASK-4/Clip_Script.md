# Task 4: The 25-Second Feature Clip Script

> **Assigned Feature**: **File to finished file, timed**  
> **Target Duration**: Exactly 25 seconds (within the 15–30s limit)  
> **Strict Rule**: *Keep numbers and counts out of it. Show what the feature does; do not quantify it.*  
> **Core Job**: *Explain it, demonstrate it, sell it.*

---

## 🎙️ Recording & Voiceover Technical Guidelines
1. **Audio Setup**: Clean cardioid microphone, zero background hum, steady speaking pace (~130 words/min).
2. **Visual Framing**:
   - Clean browser window centered on `use.superdocs.app`.
   - Dark theme enabled for high visual contrast.
   - Zero terminal popups, zero personal bookmarks, zero sensitive account details in view.
3. **No Metric Claims**: Explicitly avoided all numeric claims ("2x faster", "saved 4 hours", "10 pages in 1 minute") as strictly required by the brief.
